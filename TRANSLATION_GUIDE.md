# Ronde translation guide

All story text (MSG, EVENT, END, DEADMSG) is translated directly from the Japanese. This file fixes names,
terms and voices so every scene reads the same; follow it when editing or extending the translation.

## Background

Ronde (輪舞曲, Atlus / Multimedia Intelligence Transfer, 1997) is a Megami Tensei spin-off: a hex-grid strategy
game with SMT demons, conductors, contracts and Magnetite. Demon names follow the series' English spellings.
Story in short: in 2008 a crystal statue from Aztec ruins carries a "demon regeneration program". Decoding it on a
university supercomputer spawns demons and virtual spaces (network cities, each a netherworld) on the network.
Moloch abducts Asuka's brother Satoshi at the Ueno museum. Demons fight to be reborn as stronger beings, and
people too are reborn: past lives (Yoshitsune and Benkei, Hans and Joanna, the Aztec / Inca scenes) surface in
the cast. Satoshi becomes a demon king through child sacrifice. Asuka kills him, then must choose: stay, or go to
the Demon World and be reborn as the Demon God Angra Mainyu, the being who made this world and scattered itself
through it for the final battle. Sawaki turns out to carry Angra Mainyu in one ending.

## Style

- Translate meaning and voice, not word order. Natural, idiomatic English; keep each line about as long as the
  Japanese (EVENT.BIN has a fixed text region: `insert_event.py` prints the bytes free).
- `・・・` is "..."; long rows of dots shrink to "..." or "......". Keep stutters and trailing-off.
- No honorifics (-kun, -san). 兄さん = "Big Brother" when Satoshi addresses Asuka.
- Characters' self-reference in third person is not used in the Japanese; don't add it.
- Japanese full stops are often missing; English sentences end with punctuation.
- The font has no `~ ♡ ♪ < > $ #`. Write "!" or nothing for ♡ / ♪.
- Story lines, not menus: never abbreviate.
- Bottom-box and inline speakers: the name comes from `names.tsv`; English holds only the speech. A second
  speaker inside one MSG block starts a line with `Name: ` (the names.tsv English). EVENT blocks with codes
  between text runs take `|` between parts (one part per text run of the Japanese); a part after a `{S}` menu
  is one option per line.
- System lines: "Obtained System Disk 1." / "Asuka equipped the Ancient Sword!"
- Size limits: an MSG block may not exceed the longest original message (543 bytes; insert_msg says
  "NOT INSERTED" otherwise). EVENT.BIN and END.BIN have fixed text regions (insert_event prints bytes free).
  Last words (DEADMSG) are rows of `sys_strings.tsv`, patched in place, so each line has its own byte limit.

## Voices

- **Moloch, Aeacus, Abaddon, Susano-o, Athena**: archaic, formal ("thou" is too much; use "you", but elevated
  syntax: "So you have come.").
- **Aeacus** addresses the Demon God-to-be as "my lord", courtly.
- **Imp**: cheeky kid, "Kee-ho!" (キーホー). In END "ヒーホー" = "Hee-ho!".
- **Pixie**: chirpy. **Azazel**: pompous, smitten with Lilim. **Lilim**: haughty seductress.
- **Shopkeeper**: blustery old Tokyo working-class: "ain't", dropped g's sparingly, "you bonehead".
- **Monster-type demons** (オレサマ, katakana): broken caveman English ("Me happy! You happy!").
- **Satoshi** as demon king: cold, mocking; 僕 = whiny, 俺 = domineering.
